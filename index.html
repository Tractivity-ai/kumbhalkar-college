Social work college in Sevagram

A campus that once spun khadi now trains social workers.

Wardha is Gandhi's Sevabhoomi. Our campus on Sevagram Road began as a Khadi and Gramudyog workstation. Since 1993, the same ground has prepared students for a life of service.
